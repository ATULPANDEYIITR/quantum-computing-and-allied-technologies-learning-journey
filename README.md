# Quantum Computing and Allied Technologies Learning Journey

A structured learning journey to understand **quantum computing and the broader field of quantum technologies**, starting from the fundamentals and gradually progressing toward advanced concepts.

This repository will document my learning day by day, with each stage building upon the concepts learned previously.

## Goals

* Build a strong foundation in the mathematics required for quantum computing.
* Understand the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics relevant to computing.
* Understand how quantum information differs from classical information.
* Learn how qubits represent and process information.
* Understand quantum states, superposition, measurement, interference, and entanglement.
* Learn quantum gates and quantum circuits.
* Understand the principles behind quantum algorithms.
* Learn how quantum computers are programmed.
* Develop an understanding of quantum computational complexity.
* Understand quantum error correction and fault-tolerant quantum computing.
* Learn about different quantum computing hardware technologies.
* Understand quantum communication and quantum networking.
* Explore quantum cryptography and quantum-secure communication.
* Learn the fundamentals of quantum machine learning.
* Understand quantum simulation and quantum optimization.
* Explore quantum sensing and other emerging quantum technologies.
* Understand the relationship between quantum computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and classical computing.
* Develop the ability to read and understand introductory technical and research material related to quantum technologies.
